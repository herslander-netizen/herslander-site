Luca Radez, creative director, art director and web designer. Brand identity, web design and AI automation. Ibiza · Barcelona · Palermo.

Identities

  • QuartierConcept · 2026 It all starts with a Q drawn as a moon: the mark of Quartier, a concept project for a Parisian croissanterie. The logotype is set in Basteleur Bold, a bold display serif, alongside three more typefaces with clear roles and a warm three-colour palette (Beurre, Bleu de Nuit, Croûte). Then premium packaging (bags, étuis, cups and seal), an outdoor and magazine campaign, the menu, website and social. The whole project →
  • MeridioConcept · 2026 Green like the dial, gold like the case: from two colours comes Meridio, a fine watchmaking house imagined from scratch. The logotype is a high-contrast serif, the typefaces are three (the serif, a calligraphic script and a geometric sans for text), and the emblem is a winged ibex drawn like an engraving. The palette adds ivory, sand and near black, and the system reaches the presentation box, the ribbon, the campaign and social. The whole project →
  • KageConcept · 2026 A four-letter name deserved a typeface of its own: Kage Display, drawn for an organic Japanese matcha and set in six versions of the logo. Around it, three more typefaces, a palette of six greens and a lime, patterns and illustrations. The concept project reaches the tin, the pouches and cups, the shop sign, print, campaign and social. The whole project →
  • AlbaConcept · 2026 A sun rising over the sea, reduced to a logo you can draw from memory: that is where Alba, the identity of an airline, begins. Alba Display is a typeface drawn for the brand, working with Poppins and a pixel face for times and gates; the palette is made of morning blues, with yellow only where it matters. The system covers the airport (signage and hangar), the app, boarding passes, merchandise, the cabin and the campaign. The whole project →
